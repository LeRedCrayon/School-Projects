# Project One Storyboard | Text-Based Adventure Game

> > 

## Theme and Storyline

**Theme:**

A sci-fi mystery aboard a drifting spaceship. One of the five crewmates has
been infected by a mind-controlling parasite, and the player must figure out
who it is before the ship reaches home.

**Storyline:**

The player and five crewmates are drifting home after a long mission when one
crewmate becomes infected by a mind-controlling parasite. The player must
search the ship's rooms to gather personal belongings that serve as evidence,
clearing crewmates one by one, before running into the infected crewmate. The
final piece of evidence, a bloodstained scarf, reveals that Nadia is the one
who is infected. If the player collects all six items before entering the
Reactor Room, they survive and expose the threat. If they enter the Reactor
Room first, Nadia (Infected) catches them and the game ends.

## Rooms

Project One requires a minimum of eight rooms.

1. Crew Quarters (start room)
2. Bridge
3. Observation Deck
4. Mess Hall
5. Med Bay
6. Engine Room
7. Cargo Bay
8. Reactor Room (villain room)



## Items

With the minimum eight-room design, Project One requires at least six items.
Every room except the start room and villain room must contain one item.

1. Stuffed rabbit (Jess's) — Bridge
2. Old film camera (Devon's) — Observation Deck
3. Harmonica (the player's) — Mess Hall
4. MP3 player (Marcus's) — Med Bay
5. Potted plant (Priya's) — Engine Room
6. Bloodstained scarf (Nadia's) — Cargo Bay

## Villain

Nadia (Infected) — once a trusted crewmate, Nadia has been taken over by a
mind-controlling parasite. She waits in the Reactor Room. Finding her
bloodstained scarf in the Cargo Bay is the clue that reveals her name has
changed to "Nadia (Infected)," confirming she is the threat.
